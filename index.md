# Computational Biologist

Cancer genomics · Single-cell & multi-omics · Reproducible bioinformatics

I develop computational methods and reproducible workflows to investigate
tumour heterogeneity, cell-state plasticity, and cancer progression from
high-dimensional molecular data.

## Research

My work focuses on computational cancer biology, transcriptomics,
single-cell analysis, multi-omics integration and statistical genomics.
